A bicycle has two wheels. It is also called a bike. You power it with your legs. 
You push the pedals. This turns the wheels. It is a simple machine. It is efficient.
It is used for transport. It is used for exercise. It is used for sport. Bicycles 
were invented in the 1800s. The early ones had no pedals. You pushed with your feet
on the ground. Then the penny-farthing had a big front wheel. It was dangerous. 

The safety bicycle came later. It looks like modern bikes. It has a chain. It has two 
same-sized wheels. This design is still used. Bicycles are very common. There are 
over a billion in the world. More than cars. In some cities, many people bike to 
work. It is good for the environment. It makes no pollution. It reduces traffic. 

It is healthy exercise. Bicycling is good for your heart. It is good for your legs.
There are different types of bikes. Road bikes are light and fast. They have thin 
tires. Mountain bikes are strong. They have thick tires. They have shock absorbers.
They are for rough trails. Hybrid bikes are for city and path. Comfort bikes are for 
casual riding. BMX bikes are for tricks. Racing bikes are for competition. 

Bicycle racing is a sport. The Tour de France is a famous race. It is very hard. 
Riders go up mountains. They ride very fast. There is also track racing. This is 
on a special oval. Bicycles have many parts. The frame is the main body. It is often
metal. Aluminum or steel. Carbon fiber is light and expensive. The handlebars steer. 
The brakes stop the bike. The gears help on hills. More gears make it easier to pedal 
uphill.

Bicycles need maintenance. Tires need air. Chains need oil. Brakes need checking. 
You should wear a helmet. A helmet protects your head. It is important for safety. Many 
places have bike lanes. These are for cyclists. They keep them safe from cars. Bike 
sharing is in many cities. You can rent a bike for a short time.
