Coffee is a drink made from roasted beans. The beans come from a coffee plant.
 The coffee plant is a shrub. It grows in tropical places. 
 The two main types are Arabica and Robusta. Arabica tastes smoother. Robusta 
 has more caffeine. The beans are inside red berries. The berries are picked. 
 The beans are removed. They are then dried. After drying, they are roasted. 
 Roasting makes them brown. Roasting creates the flavor. Light roast is milder.
 Dark roast is stronger. The roasted beans are ground. Ground coffee is brewed. 
 Brewing uses hot water. The water goes through the grounds. This makes the 
 drink coffee. Coffee is usually served hot. Iced coffee is also popular. 
 Coffee contains caffeine. Caffeine is a stimulant. It makes people feel awake. 
 Many people drink coffee in the morning. It is a daily habit for millions.
 Coffee shops are common. Starbucks is a famous chain. People meet in coffee shops. 
 They talk or work there. Coffee can be prepared many ways. Espresso is strong and 
 small. Cappuccino has milk foam. Latte has steamed milk. Black coffee is just water
 and coffee. Sugar and cream are often added. Coffee is grown in many countries. 
Brazil is the biggest producer. Vietnam and Colombia also produce a lot. 
 The coffee industry is huge. It employs many farmers. Fair trade coffee tries to 
 help farmers. It pays them better prices. Coffee was discovered a long time ago. 
 The story says goats ate the berries. The herder saw the goats get energetic. He 
 tried the berries himself. Coffee drinking spread from Africa. It went to the
 Middle East. Then to Europe. Then to the rest of the world. Coffee houses became
 popular in cities. They were places for discussion. Some governments banned them. 
 They feared people plotting. But coffee stayed popular. Today, coffee is a global 
 business. Instant coffee is quick. Just add hot water. Pod machines are also convenient. 
 Coffee has health effects. Studies say it might be good. It might help the liver. 
 It might help the brain. Too much can cause jitters. It can disturb sleep. Decaf coffee has no caffeine.
